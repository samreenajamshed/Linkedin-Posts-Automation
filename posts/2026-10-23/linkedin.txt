Day 9 of AI in Plain English: what changes when AI stops talking and starts doing.

In simple words: an AI agent is an assistant that can click buttons, not just talk.

A chatbot tells you how to book the meeting.
An agent opens your calendar, finds a slot and drafts the invite.

That makes agents useful for boring, multi-step tasks at work.
But a mistake becomes an action, not just a wrong sentence.

My rule: let it prepare. You approve anything that sends, pays or deletes.
