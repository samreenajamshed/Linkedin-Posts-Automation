Day 12 of AI in Plain English: AI can read a photo of your whiteboard and turn it into notes.

In simple words: multimodal AI can see, hear and speak, not just read what you type.

At work, you can share a screenshot of an error, a photo of a receipt or a voice note, and ask about it.

One caution: AI can misread blurry photos or messy handwriting. Check names and numbers before you use them.
