April and May 2012: the highest dispatch in Coke Pakistan's history.
The lesson I kept was not about stock. It was about the dock.

In peak season everyone asks for more trucks and more space.
But a warehouse can only ship as fast as it can load.

Supply chain 101: dock throughput.
How many trucks your loading bays can turn in a day.

The rule:
Trucks per day = bays x loading hours ÷ hours per truck

Illustrative numbers:
6 bays x 20 hours ÷ 1.25 hours per truck = 96 trucks a day
Cut loading to 1 hour per truck: 6 x 20 ÷ 1 = 120 trucks
That is 25% more dispatch with no new bay and no new truck.

That same May we reached 100% palletised dispatch. Pallets load faster than loose cases.

Common mistake: buying trucks or space when the queue is at the dock.
A truck waiting to load is capacity you already paid for.

Monday morning:
→ Time 10 truck loads, gate in to gate out
→ Split it: waiting, loading, paperwork
→ Fix the biggest slice first

Which slice eats most of your loading time?
