Supplier B quoted 6% less. It cost more to land.

Price is what you pay the supplier. Landed cost is what you pay to have it on your shelf.

Landed cost: every cost to get one unit into your warehouse, ready to use.
Landed cost per unit = price + freight + duties + insurance + handling, all ÷ units received

Illustrative numbers, per unit:
→ Supplier A: 100 price + 4 freight + 10 duty + 2 handling = 116
→ Supplier B: 94 price + 11 freight + 9 duty + 4 handling = 118

The cheaper quote is the dearer buy. A longer lead time often adds safety stock too, and that is cash.

The common mistake: comparing quotes on price alone, then watching freight and port bills break the budget.

Monday morning: take your top 5 purchased items and redo the supplier comparison on landed cost.

Which cost surprises you most when you land it: freight, duty or handling?
