Your warehouse rent is the cheapest part of holding stock.
Most teams count 5% a year and miss the other 20%.

In stock reviews, the talk is about space and handling.
The bigger bill sits in finance, in lines nobody adds up.

Supply chain 101: inventory carrying cost.
What it costs per year to keep stock on hand, not to buy it.

Carrying cost = average inventory value x carrying cost rate

Illustrative numbers, average stock of PKR 50 million:
→ Cost of cash tied up: 12%
→ Storage and handling: 5%
→ Insurance and tax: 2%
→ Shrinkage and damage: 2%
→ Expiry and obsolescence: 4%

Rate = 25%. Carrying cost = PKR 12.5 million a year.
Count storage and handling alone and you see 2.5 million.

The common mistake: treating extra stock as "safe". In FMCG, every extra day of cover spends cash, space and shelf life.

What to do this week:
1. Agree your carrying cost rate with finance, in writing.
2. Put it next to every stock target in S&OP.
3. Price the slow movers first.
