Demand barely moved. Every order upstream went wild.
That's the bullwhip effect, and most of it is self-made.

Bullwhip effect: small swings in real sales grow bigger at every step back up the chain.

Bullwhip ratio = swing in orders placed ÷ swing in real sales
Above 1 means you are amplifying demand, not passing it on.

Illustrative numbers:
→ Shop sales move 5% week to week
→ Distributor orders move 15%
→ Plant schedule moves 30%
→ Preform and sleeve orders move 50%

Same shoppers. Ten times the noise at the far end.

What cracks the whip:
1. Ordering in big batches
2. Month-end loading and promotions
3. Every link forecasting from orders, not sales

Monday morning: put distributor sell-out next to their orders for the last 8 weeks. If orders swing harder, plan from sell-out.
