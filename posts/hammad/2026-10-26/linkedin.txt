Trucks idle on the 5th. Queuing at the dock on the 28th.
That's not demand. That's a month-end habit.

Most FMCG warehouses know the rush. Sales chase the monthly target, orders pile into the last week, and dispatch pays for it: overtime, hired trucks, rushed loading, short shipments.

Teach: Dispatch linearity.
How evenly you ship across the month.

Last-week share = cases dispatched in the final week ÷ cases dispatched in the month x 100

In a 4-week month, flat is about 25%.

Illustrative numbers: 100,000 cases in the month, 40,000 in the last week.
40,000 ÷ 100,000 = 40%.
Your dock, fleet and crew get sized for 40,000 a week, then sit half idle at 20,000 in week 1.

Common mistake: adding trucks for month-end. That pays for the peak instead of flattening it.

Monday morning:
→ Track dispatch by week, not just by month
→ Agree weekly volume targets with sales, not only a monthly one
→ Book fixed loading slots for big customers early in the month

What share of your month ships in the last week?
