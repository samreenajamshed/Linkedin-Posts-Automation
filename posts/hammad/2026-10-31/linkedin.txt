Most stockouts are planning problems.
Often the supplier delivered on time. The order went out late.

Reorder point: the stock level that tells you to order now, not when the shelf looks low.

Reorder point = daily demand x lead time in days + safety stock

Illustrative numbers:
200 cases a day x 5 days + 300 cases safety stock = 1,300 cases.
When stock hits 1,300, the order goes.

The common mistake: setting it once and forgetting it.
Demand moves. Lead time moves. The trigger must move with them.

Monday morning: take your top 20 SKUs and recalculate with the last 8 weeks of demand and the lead time you actually got.
