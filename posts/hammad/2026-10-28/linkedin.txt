Your system says lead time is 14 days.
The real number is 21. Nobody told the plan.

Most teams store one lead time: the supplier's quote.
Stock arrives on four clocks, not one.

Replenishment lead time: the full time from deciding to buy until stock can be picked.

Lead time = approval + supplier + transit + dock-to-stock

Illustrative numbers:
→ PO approval: 3 days
→ Supplier: 10 days
→ Transit: 6 days
→ Dock-to-stock: 2 days

Total: 21 days. The system plans on 14.
At 100 cases a day, that is 700 cases of demand the reorder point never covered.

The common mistake: blaming the supplier when the missing days sit in your own approvals and your own receiving dock.

Monday morning: take your top 10 SKUs, time the last 5 receipts of each from PO request to pickable, and update the master data.

Which of the four clocks runs longest in your operation?
