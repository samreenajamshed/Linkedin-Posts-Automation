A 98% full warehouse is not efficient.
It's a warehouse with no room for tomorrow's trucks.

On a cost report, empty pallet positions look like waste. On the floor, they are your working space.

Storage headroom: the free space left for what arrives next.
Headroom = usable pallet positions minus occupied positions.

Illustrative numbers: 1,000 positions, 980 full, so 20 free.
Tomorrow 120 pallets arrive. 100 of them sit in the aisle and get handled twice.

The fix: check headroom against your biggest inbound day, not utilisation alone.

What utilisation do you treat as full?
