Four KPIs at 95%. Your customer sees 83%.

Transport is proud of on time. The warehouse is proud of fill. Finance is happy with invoices.

The customer only sees one order. It has to be right at every step.

One concept worth knowing: perfect order rate.
The share of orders that arrive on time, in full, undamaged and with a correct invoice. All four, on the same order.

Perfect order rate = on time % × in full % × damage free % × invoice accuracy %

Illustrative numbers:
→ On time 95%
→ In full 93%
→ Damage free 98%
→ Invoice right 96%
0.95 × 0.93 × 0.98 × 0.96 = 83%

So about 1 order in 6 fails somewhere, while every team reports green.

The common mistake: averaging the four. That gives 95.5% and hides the problem. Failures stack, they don't average.

What I'd do Monday morning:
1. Score a sample of last week's orders against all four checks.
2. Find the check that fails most often.
3. Fix that one first. The weakest gate caps the whole chain.

Which of the four fails most often in your operation?
