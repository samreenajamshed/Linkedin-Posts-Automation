I've helped take SAP live twice.
The software was never the hard part.

At Engro Foods, it was SAP MM and LE in a dairy warehouse in Sukkur. At Pepsi Bottling Ventures in Faisalabad, a re-implementation.

What both taught me: the real test comes after go-live. Does the system stock match what's on the rack?

Teach: Inventory record accuracy, IRA.
The share of counted locations where system stock matches physical stock.

IRA = locations that match ÷ locations counted x 100

Illustrative numbers: count 200 locations, 170 match.
170 ÷ 200 = 85% IRA.
So 1 location in 7 is wrong, and every plan built on it inherits the error.

Common mistake: netting. Plus 30 cases in one bay and minus 30 in another looks like zero variance. IRA scores them as two misses.

Monday morning:
→ Count 20 fast-moving locations, not the whole warehouse
→ Score match or no match, no netting
→ Fix the step behind each miss, not just the number
