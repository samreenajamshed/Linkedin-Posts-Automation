Two hours of S&OP. Not one decision written down.
Next month, the same debate starts again.

Running national S&OP in a volatile edible-oil market taught me this: a meeting without a written decision is just a conversation.

AI now does the boring part in minutes.

Paste the meeting transcript or your rough notes and ask:
"From these S&OP notes, list every decision made. For each one give the decision, the owner, the due date and the KPI it affects. Flag any decision with no owner or no date."

What you get: a draft decision log before people leave the room.

The concept behind it, the S&OP decision log:
→ One line per decision: what, who, by when.
→ Action closure rate = actions closed on time ÷ actions due x 100
→ Illustrative numbers: 18 actions due, 12 closed on time = 67%.

If that rate keeps falling, the problem is the process, not the forecast.

The check: AI drafts, the S&OP owner confirms every line. Transcripts carry prices and volumes, so use only a company-approved tool.
