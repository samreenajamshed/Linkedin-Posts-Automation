Same cases shipped. 20 more labour hours paid.

When the dock falls behind, the first answer is usually overtime. Often the real gap is productivity, not headcount.

Labour productivity: how much work each paid hour produces.
Cases per labour hour = cases handled ÷ paid labour hours

Illustrative numbers:
→ Shift A: 12,000 cases ÷ 80 hours = 150 per hour
→ Shift B: 12,000 cases ÷ 100 hours = 120 per hour

Before approving overtime, find where the 20 hours went: waiting for trucks, searching for stock, walking empty.
