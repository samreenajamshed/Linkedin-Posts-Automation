Change the plan inside the frozen week and you pay twice.
Once on the line, then again on the orders you bumped.

The fix is a planning time fence: an agreed line in the calendar that says how freely the plan may change.

→ Frozen: the next 1 to 2 weeks. A change needs a named approver.
→ Slushy: weeks 3 to 4. Changes inside agreed limits only.
→ Liquid: beyond that. Replan freely.

How to track it:
Schedule adherence = batches made as planned ÷ batches planned.
Illustrative numbers: 34 of 40 batches = 85%.

On Monday, log every fence break with one reason and who approved it.
Repeat reasons point to a process gap, not a planning one.
