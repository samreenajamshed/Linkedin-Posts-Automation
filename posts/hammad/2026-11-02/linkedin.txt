Your SOPs are signed. Your floor isn't following them.
A full training register proves attendance, not behaviour.

The fix is never a thicker binder.

When I built activity-level SOPs for warehousing and logistics, three rules mattered:
→ Write each SOP with the people who do the job
→ One page per activity, photos over paragraphs
→ Check it on the floor, not in the register

The number to track: SOP adherence rate.
In plain English: how often the job is done the way the SOP says, when someone actually watches.

SOP adherence rate = tasks done fully to standard ÷ tasks observed

Illustrative numbers: a supervisor watches 20 put-aways this week. 14 follow every step. Adherence is 70%.
The 6 misses show you where the SOP is unclear, not who to blame.

Common mistake: reporting training sign-offs as compliance. People can sign on Monday and skip a step on Tuesday.

Monday morning: pick one activity, watch 10 tasks, and fix the step most people skip.
