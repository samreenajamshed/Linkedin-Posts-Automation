Every planner knows the Monday routine: open PO list, copy, paste, chase, repeat.
The chasing matters. The copy paste does not.

Teach: Supplier OTIF, on time in full.
The share of PO lines that arrive by the confirmed date and in the full ordered quantity.

Supplier OTIF = PO lines on time and in full ÷ PO lines due
Illustrative numbers: 40 lines due, 30 on time and in full → 30 ÷ 40 = 75%.
Common mistake: counting POs, not lines. One short line hides inside a delivered PO.

The prompt:
"Group these open PO lines by supplier. Flag lines past due or due in 7 days. Draft one short chase email per supplier listing each line, quantity and date needed. Rank suppliers by OTIF on this data."

Monday morning:
→ Export open PO lines with confirmed dates, no pricing
→ Run the prompt, check each line against your ERP
→ Send the emails yourself, and call the worst supplier

AI drafts the chase. The relationship stays yours.
