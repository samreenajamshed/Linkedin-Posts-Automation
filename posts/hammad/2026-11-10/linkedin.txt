Your ageing report has 400 lines.
You only need to act on 10 of them.

Most teams sort ageing by oldest stock first. That's the wrong list.
Old stock that sells fast is fine. Younger stock that sells slowly is the real write-off.

The concept: stock at expiry risk.
The cases that won't sell before customers stop accepting them.

Days left to sell = days to expiry minus customer minimum shelf life
Cases at risk = stock on hand minus average daily sales x days left to sell

Illustrative numbers:
1,800 cases, 75 days to expiry, customers need 45 days left.
30 days left to sell x 40 cases a day = 1,200 cases.
600 cases at risk. Act this week, not at month end.

Where AI saves the hour: paste the ageing report with sales by SKU and batch, then ask:
"Calculate cases at risk for each SKU and batch. Rank the top 10 by value at risk. Suggest one action for each: transfer, promote or slow production."

The check: shelf life and sales data must be right first, and a human signs off every action. Keep customer and cost data out of public tools.

Monday morning → run it on your slowest depot.
