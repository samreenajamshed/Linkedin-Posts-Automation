A warehouse KPI read at 5 PM is a post-mortem.
Read it before 9 AM and it becomes the plan for the day.

The slides show the 5 numbers I'd put on a morning board at the dock. One of them needs a closer look.

Pick accuracy: the share of order lines picked with the right item, quantity and batch.

Pick accuracy = correct lines ÷ lines picked x 100

Illustrative numbers: 1,970 correct of 2,000 lines = 98.5%.
Sounds good. It can still mean 30 deliveries with a problem today.

Common mistake: counting errors only when a customer complains. Check at the staging lane, before the truck door closes.

Monday morning: one board, 5 numbers, and the shift supervisor explains each one.

Which KPI would you add to your morning board?
