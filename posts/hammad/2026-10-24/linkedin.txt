Late dispatch rarely needs more trucks.
It usually needs more trips from the trucks you already have.

Trips per vehicle: how many loaded trips each truck makes in a working day.
Trips per vehicle = total trips ÷ trucks in use

Illustrative numbers: 20 trucks, 30 trips a day = 1.5.
Lift it to 2 and you get 10 extra trips a day, with zero new trucks.

Where the lost trip usually hides:
→ trucks queuing at the dock for loads
→ order cutoffs that drift later every week
→ long unloading waits at the customer

Fix those before you sign for another truck.
