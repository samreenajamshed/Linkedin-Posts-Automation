Target: 60% palletised dispatch.
By May 2012 we were at 100%.

Palletisation looks like an equipment problem. Most of the work is making pallet-first the default at the loading bay, not the special case.

Palletised dispatch rate: the share of cases that leave on a pallet instead of being hand-loaded.

Palletised dispatch rate = cases dispatched on pallets ÷ total cases dispatched

Illustrative numbers: 9,000 of 12,000 cases on pallets = 75%.

Why it pays: fewer touches, faster loading, less damage, quicker counts.

Common mistake: counting pallets, not cases. You can ship plenty of pallets and still hand-load most of your volume.
