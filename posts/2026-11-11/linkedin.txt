Day 17 of AI in Plain English: how AI apps reach your files and your tools.

In simple words: frameworks like LangChain are the plumbing between the AI and everything else.

The AI model is the brain. On its own it can't open your files, search a database or send an email.

A framework connects the pipes: fetch the data, hand it to the AI, run the tool, return the answer.

Developers use them so they don't build every pipe from scratch. You never see the plumbing, only the tap.
