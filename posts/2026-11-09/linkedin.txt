Day 16 of AI in Plain English: why some AI is free to download and some isn't.

In simple words: closed AI is eating out, open AI is cooking at home.

Closed models like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini run on the company's servers. A polished meal, but no recipe.

Open models like Llama, Mistral and Gemma can be downloaded and run on your own computers. More work, but your data can stay in-house.

Neither is better. It depends on how private your data is and who will run it.
