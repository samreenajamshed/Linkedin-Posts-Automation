Finance folks: the hard part of reconciliation was never the lines that match.

At IFFCO Pakistan, I built reconciliation pipelines across 12+ distributors.

Most lines matched on their own. The real work hid in the few that didn't: a missing credit note, a price change one side missed, an invoice booked twice.

The rule I still use with AI:
→ Let the tool sort the matches.
→ Spend your time on the exceptions.

Try it on your next vendor statement. Paste your ledger and the statement into ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini: "Compare these two lists. Show only the lines that don't match, as a table with date, amount and a likely reason."

Check each flagged line against the source yourself. AI points, you decide.

Remove account numbers and names first, or use your company-approved tool.
