Teachers, one lesson for 30 different learners is the hardest part of the job.

Friends who teach often ask me where to start with AI. My answer comes from 7+ years in data work.

The hard part was rarely the numbers. It was explaining one result to a CEO and to a warehouse team, in words each of them could follow.

Your classroom has that problem every day.

So start here. Paste one explanation you already teach and add:
"You are a helpful teaching assistant. Rewrite this for my grade 7 class at three levels: simpler, on level and stretch. Keep the key facts the same. Add 2 check questions for each level."

Why it works: you bring the content and the judgment. AI only does the rewriting.

Read every version before class, and never paste student names or marks.
