HR friends keep asking me where to start with AI.

After 7+ years building data and AI systems, my answer never changes.

Start with writing. Not deciding.

AI is great at first drafts:
→ a job ad from your messy notes
→ 8 interview questions for one specific role
→ a kind, clear rejection email
→ a week-one onboarding checklist

AI is risky when it decides who moves forward. It can copy bias from past hiring data, and it sounds confident even when it is wrong.

So the rule I give them: AI writes, you decide.

One safety note: never paste CVs, names or salaries into a public chatbot. Use your company-approved tool, and a human makes every hiring call.
