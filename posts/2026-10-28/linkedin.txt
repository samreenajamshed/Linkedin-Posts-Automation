Day 11 of AI in Plain English: why most teams never need to train their own AI.

In simple words: prompting is handing a chef a recipe. Fine-tuning is sending the chef to cooking school.

Prompting: you explain what you want each time. Quick and cheap.

Fine-tuning: you train it on many of your own examples so a style sticks. Slower, costs more, needs clean data.

My rule: better prompts first, then let AI read your files. Train only if both fall short.
