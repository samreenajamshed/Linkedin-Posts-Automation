Sales folks: no more scrambling 5 minutes before a client meeting.

The old way: six tabs open, old emails, scattered notes, and questions made up on the spot.

The switch: paste what you already know into ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot and ask for a one-page meeting brief. What they do, their likely needs, 5 questions to ask, and objections to expect.

Why it works: AI is good at organising messy notes. You bring the judgment and the relationship.

Use public info only, or your company-approved tool for anything from your CRM.
