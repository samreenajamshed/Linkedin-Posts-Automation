Day 10 of AI in Plain English: how AI gets a calculator, a browser and your calendar.

In simple words: tools are apps the AI is allowed to use, like kit you hand a smart new hire.

On its own, AI only writes from what it learned.
With tools, it can search today's web, run the numbers or check your diary.

When you see "Searching the web" in ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot, that's a tool at work.

My tip: only switch on the tools a task needs, especially email and files.
