Small business owners: stop writing every product description from scratch.

The old way: a blank page for each item, borrowed wording from a competitor, the same tired words on every listing.

The AI way: paste your own facts, size, material, price and who it's for, into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Ask for 3 short versions in your shop's tone. Pick one and tweak it.

Why it works: AI writes from your facts, so it sounds like your shop, not a template.

One rule: never let it add claims you can't back up, like "waterproof" or "organic".
