Day 14 of AI in Plain English: why AI can be unfair without meaning to be.

In simple words: AI is a mirror of the examples it learned from.

If most past examples of a "good manager" were men, AI may lean that way too.

You can see this in CV screening, loan checks, even the pictures AI draws.

My rule at work: treat AI's answer as a draft, not a verdict. Ask who might be left out, and let a human make the final call.
