Real estate agents: the weekly seller update doesn't have to eat your Friday.

The old way: scroll through chats and notes from 6 viewings, then try to turn them into a polite, honest email.

With AI: paste your viewing notes into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot. Ask for a short update covering what buyers liked, the main concerns, and one suggested next step.

Why it works: AI is good at sorting messy notes into themes. You still check every line and add your own advice.

Remove buyers' names and phone numbers before you paste.
