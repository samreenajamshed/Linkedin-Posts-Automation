Managers: before you hand anything to AI, find the task your team dreads.

At Gerry's Group, I built the BI function from zero. One of the first things I fixed was a reporting cycle that took two days of manual work.

That fix used data pipelines, not AI. But the lesson is the one I still use to pick tasks for AI today. Start with a task that:
→ repeats every week or month
→ follows the same steps each time
→ a person can check quickly

Tick all three and AI can take the first draft off your plate. If the task needs judgment about people, keep it human.

The right first task matters more than the right tool.

What is the one task your team dreads every week?
