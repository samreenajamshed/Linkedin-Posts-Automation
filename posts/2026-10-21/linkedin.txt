Day 8 of AI in Plain English: how AI finds the right page even when you use different words.

In simple words: a library sorted by meaning, not by title.

Ask "how many days off do I get?" and it still finds the page called "Annual leave policy".

AI turns each sentence into numbers that capture its meaning, called embeddings. Similar meanings sit close together.

A vector database stores them and finds the closest match fast.

It's the search behind Day 7's RAG, so clean, current files matter.
