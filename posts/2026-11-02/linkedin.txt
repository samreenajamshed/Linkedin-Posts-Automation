Day 13 of AI in Plain English: why AI gives confident wrong answers from messy files.

In simple words: garbage in, confident garbage out.

AI can't tell that your sheet has duplicate rows, old prices or blank cells. It reads what you give it and answers as if it's all true.

So a messy sales file becomes a neat summary with wrong numbers.

Before asking AI about your data, clean it: remove duplicates, fix dates, delete old tabs.

In one of my data roles, adding checks before reports went out cut reporting errors by 35%.
