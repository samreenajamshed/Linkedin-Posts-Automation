Support teams: you answer the same questions every week. Turn them into help articles with AI.

Here's how to do it without creating new problems:

→ Paste your own fix notes and 3 real tickets, with names and order numbers removed.
→ Ask for short, numbered steps in plain words.
→ Follow every step yourself before you publish.
→ Never let AI guess a policy, price or deadline. Link the official page.

Why it works: AI is good at structure and plain language. Your team knows what actually fixes the problem.
