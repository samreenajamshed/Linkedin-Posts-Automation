Marketers: if your AI drafts all sound the same, this is why.

You ask for "a post about our new product". AI gives you the average of the internet.

The fix is simple:
→ Paste 3 posts you wrote and say "match this voice"
→ Give it the audience, the goal and the length
→ Ask for 3 options, then edit the best one

Why it works: AI copies whatever examples you give it. No examples, no voice.

Before you paste anything, remove client names and unreleased plans, or use your company-approved tool. And check every fact and number yourself.
