Freelancers: your client's files are not yours to paste into any chatbot.

AI can make you faster on client work. It can also break a client's trust in one paste.

Before you use AI on a project:
→ Check your contract or NDA for AI rules
→ Remove names, numbers and logins first
→ Read and fix every line before you send

Why it matters: some free AI tools may use what you paste to improve their models, and your client never agreed to that.

AI writes the draft. You own the result.
