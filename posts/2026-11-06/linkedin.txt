Day 15 of AI in Plain English: why AI gives you a different answer each time you ask.

In simple words: AI has a creativity dial, called temperature.

Turn it down and AI picks the safest next word. Same question, almost the same answer.

Turn it up and it takes more chances. Fresh ideas, but more slips.

Low for facts, numbers and policies. High for slogans, names and brainstorms.

Most chat apps set the dial for you. Your words still help: "be precise" or "give me 10 wild ideas".
