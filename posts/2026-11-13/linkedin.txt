Day 18 of AI in Plain English: why does ChatGPT forget you by tomorrow?

In simple words: most chatbots hand you a fresh notepad for every new chat.

Each chat starts blank. It only sees that one conversation.

Some tools now have a memory feature. They save short notes about you, like your job or preferred tone, and read them before each new chat.

Handy, but check what it saved. You can view and delete those notes in settings. Keep confidential details out.
